Caring for your child

Explain what will happen in simple words, bring a favorite toy or blanket, and ask our child life specialists for help with fear of needles or scans.

Medical records

Parents and guardians can ask for records through the portal or by calling health information management at (555) 010-0145. In this replica the request form is not included. The portal page is a demo.
