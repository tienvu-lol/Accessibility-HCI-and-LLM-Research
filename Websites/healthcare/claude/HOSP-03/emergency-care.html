Emergency care for kids

Know when to call 911 and where to go.

In a life-threatening emergency, call 911

Call 911 if your child is struggling to breathe, is unresponsive, has a seizure that does not stop, has severe bleeding, or you think they may have swallowed poison. Poison control in the US: 1-800-222-1222.

When to go to the emergency department

  • A broken bone with visible deformity or a head injury with vomiting or confusion
  • A high fever in a baby younger than 3 months
  • Trouble breathing, or lips that look blue
  • Severe pain that does not ease, or a child who is very hard to wake

For minor problems such as a mild fever or a small cut, urgent care or your child's primary care office may be faster. See the comparison table.
