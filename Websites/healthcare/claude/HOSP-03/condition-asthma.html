Asthma in children

What asthma is, what to watch for, and how our team helps.

Overview

Asthma is a long-term condition in which the airways swell and narrow, making it harder to breathe. It is one of the most common reasons children miss school. With a written plan and the right medicine, most children with asthma stay active.

Signs to watch for

  • Coughing that is worse at night or during exercise
  • A whistling sound when breathing out
  • Chest tightness or fast breathing
  • Symptoms that follow colds, smoke, pollen or pets

Call 911 if your child cannot speak in full sentences, has blue lips, or their rescue medicine is not helping.

Common questions

A one-page plan that lists daily medicines, rescue steps and when to call the doctor. Share it with school and caregivers.

Some children improve with age and others do not. Regular visits help the team adjust treatment as your child grows.

Doctors use symptom history, a physical exam and, for older children, a breathing test called spirometry.
