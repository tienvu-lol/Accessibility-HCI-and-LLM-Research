Heart & Vascular Institute

Prevention, diagnosis and treatment for conditions of the heart and blood vessels.

Our approach

Teams of specialists, nurses and therapists meet regularly to plan your care, so you hear one plan instead of many opinions. All details on this page are fictional.

Conditions and services

  • Arrhythmia care

    Monitoring and treatment of irregular heart rhythms.

  • Heart failure clinic

    Medicine adjustment and education.

  • Cardiac rehabilitation

    Supervised exercise after a heart event.

  • Vascular care

    Evaluation of leg pain, aneurysms and clots.
