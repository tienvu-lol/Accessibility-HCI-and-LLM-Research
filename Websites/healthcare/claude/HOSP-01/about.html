About Harborlight Health

A fictional academic medical center created for an accessibility and usability research dataset.

Who we are

Harborlight Health combines patient care, research and education. All names, places and numbers on this site are invented.

Quality of patient care

A real health system would publish quality and safety measures here. This replica has no such data.

Leadership

A real site would list executives and board members. Leadership profiles are intentionally omitted in this replica.

Clinical trials and research

Research studies are described to patients by their care team. No studies are listed in this replica.

Education and training

The fictional Harborlight schools of medicine, nursing and pharmacy are described only in this sentence.

Accessibility resources

We aim to make this site usable with a keyboard and assistive technology. Language and disability services at a real hospital include interpreters, large-print materials and accessible rooms. To report a problem with this fictional site, use the contact details below.

Nondiscrimination notice

Harborlight Health provides care without discrimination based on race, color, national origin, age, disability or sex. This is sample copy, not a legal notice.
