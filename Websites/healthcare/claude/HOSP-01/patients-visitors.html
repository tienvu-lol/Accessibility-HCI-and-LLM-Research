Visitor information

Visiting hours and parking at Harborlight Medical Center (sample information):

Sample visiting hours
UnitHours
General units10 am to 8 pm
Intensive careTwo visitors at a time; check with the unit
Children’s HospitalParents and guardians at any time

Visitor parking is in the Example Way garage. Wheelchairs are available at the main entrance.

Virtual visits

Many doctors offer video visits. Use the doctor search and choose “Offers video visits”. Video visits are not available inside this replica.

Second opinion

If you want another expert to review a diagnosis or treatment plan, request an appointment and mention a second opinion in the reason field.

Refer a patient

Clinicians can call the referral line at (555) 010-0150 (fictional number). No online referral form is included in this replica.
