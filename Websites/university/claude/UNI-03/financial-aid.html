Financial Aid

Most students receive some form of help paying for college.

  • Admissions & Aid
  • Apply
  • Financial Aid
  • Tuition & Costs
  • Visit & Connect

Types of aid

Need-based money you do not repay. Complete the federal aid form to be considered.

Merit and community scholarships. The sample foundation application opens each February.

Part-time campus jobs that fit around your class schedule.

Borrow only what you need. An advisor can review options with you.

Military & veterans

Veterans and service members can use education benefits at Harbor Valley. Contact the Financial Aid Office at aid@hvcc.example (sample address).

Checklist

  • Create a federal student aid account.
  • Submit the aid form for the correct year.
  • Check your student email for follow-up requests.
  • Review your estimated costs.
