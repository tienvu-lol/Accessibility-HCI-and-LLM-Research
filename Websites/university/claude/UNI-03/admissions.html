Explore admissions

Cardinal Ridge has open admission: if you have a high school diploma, an equivalent, or are a dual-enrollment student, you can enroll.

  1. Apply. Complete the application (simulated here).
  2. Apply for aid. See financial aid.
  3. Meet an advisor. Plan your first term.
  4. Register. Choose sections and pay or set up a plan.

Common questions

Do I need test scores?

No. Placement uses your transcript and a short guided self-placement (sample policy).

What is dual enrollment?

High school students can take college courses at reduced cost with counselor approval.

Information for international students

International applicants submit visa and financial documents with the application. This is placeholder guidance.
