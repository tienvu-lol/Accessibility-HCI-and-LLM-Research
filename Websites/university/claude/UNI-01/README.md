## Interaction simulation notes

- **Simulation only:** MyWren sign-in (dialog), the application-start form, the visit-request form, ticket and tool buttons, and the cost estimator. Each says it is a demo; nothing is stored, sent or authenticated. Estimator rates and the “Wren Promise” are invented.
- Footer and tool items with no local destination (Jobs, Alumni, Policies, …) are buttons that open a demo notice rather than dead links.
- The reference loads a cookie-consent script and a site-alert script; neither is reproduced.
- Sample data counts: 15 programs, 12 people, 8 events, 6 news stories, 12 searchable pages, 8 demo “Campus tools”. Event dates are fixed samples (Nov 2026–Jan 2027) and are not filtered by today’s date.

## Known limitations

- The reference’s dropdown behavior, look-ahead data source, directory API and site alerts were not observed; the replica approximates them.
- The `<dialog>` content and form error messages are created by JavaScript, so those parts need JS.
- Layout, spacing and colors are original and only loosely follow the reference’s structure; no pixel fidelity is claimed.
- Headers and footers are duplicated across pages (no include mechanism); the pages were produced with a throwaway local script that is not part of the deliverable.
