    Ways to visit

    • Virtual visit

      A self-guided overview (no live video in this demo).

    Plan your trip

    Visitor parking is behind the Welcome Center. Campus shuttles run every fifteen minutes on weekdays (fictional schedule).

    Tours can be adjusted for wheelchairs, mobility devices and sensory needs. Describe your needs in the form below.

    Several fictional hotels in Lakemont offer visitor rates. Ask the Welcome Center for the list.
