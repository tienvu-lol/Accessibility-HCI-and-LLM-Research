    Four steps from interest to enrollment.

    • Admissions & Aid

    How to apply

    1. Choose a program. Browse majors and certificates.
    2. Gather materials. Transcripts, a short essay and (for some programs) an audition or portfolio.
    3. Submit your application. Use the application portal. This replica offers a demo start page only.
    4. Watch for your decision. Decisions are posted to the applicant portal (not available here).

    Deadlines by applicant type

    Early action: November 15. Regular decision: February 1. (Fictional dates.)

    Fall term: March 15. Spring term: October 15. (Fictional dates.)

    Fall term: January 15. Include English proficiency scores and financial documents. (Fictional dates.)

    Deadlines vary by program. Contact the program coordinator listed in the directory.
