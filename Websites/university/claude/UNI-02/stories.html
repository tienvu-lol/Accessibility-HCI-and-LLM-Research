Stories from Linden Ridge

Original sample features written for this replica.

Alumni voices: four paths from one seminar table

Four graduates from different decades describe the habits they still use: reading the footnotes, asking for the data, and offering a draft before it feels ready. Each says that a small seminar taught them to disagree without leaving the room.

Field notes from a first-year lab

In a spring-term lab, first-years collected pond samples every Friday and built a shared notebook of what changed. By May they had a dataset large enough to argue about.

A season on the black-box stage

Two student directors staged a play in a room without wings. They describe the problems of entrances, quick changes and one very stubborn door.

Night shift at the reading room

Student library workers describe the quiet hours: shelving, answering questions about citation styles and watching the snow fall past the tall windows.

Students map the college's old orchard trees

Working with the grounds crew, a biology seminar tagged and mapped forty heritage fruit trees on campus and wrote a short care guide for each.

New seminar pairs poetry with data visualization

Next spring students will turn a century of local weather records into poems and charts, then exhibit both in the library gallery.
