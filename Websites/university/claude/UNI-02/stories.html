Stories from Alderbrook

Features about the people who make Alderbrook what it is (fictional).

From a capstone to a career

Priyanka Desai ’19 traces her work in clean-energy policy back to a senior project measuring the library’s electricity use. “I learned that data only matters if someone can act on it,” she says.

A semester at the creek lab

First-year students in Professor Okafor’s seminar spend Fridays collecting samples from Wren Creek. By December they present findings to the town council.

Building a dance from scratch

For the fall concert, choreographer Leo Marchetti ’27 turned field recordings of the creek into a nine-minute piece for eleven dancers.

Internships in the city

During winter term, juniors work in Quillfield’s museums, clinics and start-ups, then bring what they learn back to class.

Faculty spotlight: Gregor Anselm

Computer science professor Gregor Anselm teaches a course in which students build software with community partners, and they test it with real users who rely on assistive technology.
