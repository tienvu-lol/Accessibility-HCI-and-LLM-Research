Campus life

Where learning continues after class.

Arts

Two theaters, a gallery and open studios; the fall dance concert and winter showcase are student-run.

Athletics & recreation

Twenty-two varsity teams, the Herons, and intramural leagues anyone can join.

Be well

Counseling, health services and peer wellness coaches, with same-week appointments.

Career services

Advising from the first year, alumni mentors and a winter-term internship program.

Clubs & activities

More than ninety student-run groups, from robotics to a cappella to the garden club.

Living on campus

First-year halls are small communities near the quad; upper-class students choose among houses and apartments.

Sustainability

Solar rooftops, a campus food garden and the net-zero-by-2040 plan, with student fellows on every committee.
