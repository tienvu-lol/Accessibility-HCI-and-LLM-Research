Living on campus

All students live on campus for four years. First-year halls are organized into small neighbourhoods with a resident advisor. Meals are served in the Commons, which offers vegetarian, halal and allergen-free stations.

Sustainability

The campus sustainability plan targets net-zero emissions by 2040 (fictional). Milestones: retire the oil boilers by 2030, install rooftop solar on six buildings, and cut waste by half.

Be well

Counseling, health services and peer wellness coaches are available to all students. Services shown here are descriptions only; nothing can be scheduled in this replica.
