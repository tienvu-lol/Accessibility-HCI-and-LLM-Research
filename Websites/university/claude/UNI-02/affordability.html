Financial aid & cost

The sample cost of attendance is $78,400 for 2026–27, covering tuition, housing, meals, fees and an allowance for books and travel. Aid is offered as grants, not loans. Contact the financial aid director.

Tuition guarantee

Tuition for entering students is fixed for four years, so families can plan. The guarantee begins with the class of 2031.

Net price estimator (demonstration)

Demonstration: An intentional addition. This is a made-up formula for exercising a form with a result, not a real estimate and not an offer of aid. Nothing is stored or sent.

Common questions

Is aid need-based?

Yes. Aid is based on family financial circumstances; there are no merit awards in this fictional policy.

Do I reapply for aid each year?

Yes, with a short renewal form each spring.

What if my situation changes?

Contact the financial aid office to request a review.
