    First-year experience

    Every first-year student takes a seminar of fifteen or fewer, chosen from topics as varied as “Rivers and Cities” and “Why We Argue”, taught by a faculty member who also serves as their first advisor.

    Research

    Students can join faculty projects as early as the first year, and summer fellowships fund independent research. Many seniors present at regional conferences.
