Design your own major

When no single department fits your question, build a course of study around it.

How it works

  1. Explore. Talk with your first-year advisor about the question you want to study.
  2. Find two advisors. Choose faculty from at least two departments who will guide the plan.
  3. Write the proposal. Describe the question, list at least twelve courses and outline a senior project.
  4. Submit by the deadline. The curriculum committee reviews proposals in the spring of the sophomore year (see the calendar).

Common questions

Who can apply?

Any sophomore in good standing who has found at least two advisors.

Can the major include study abroad?

Yes. Up to four courses from approved programs can count.

Examples of past majors

Sample (fictional) titles: Urban Food Systems, Music and Cognition, Computational Linguistics for Public Health.
