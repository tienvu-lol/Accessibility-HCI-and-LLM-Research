Meet Alderbrook

A small college with big questions: what we value, where we came from and how we work together.

Mission

Alderbrook educates students to think carefully, act generously and take part in public life. We pair rigorous study with hands-on work in the community around us.

Why liberal arts?

Breadth teaches students to ask better questions. A biology major might study poetry and a philosophy major might run an experiment, and both leave knowing how to learn.

A brief history

Founded in 1889 by a group of Quillfield educators, Alderbrook opened with forty students and two buildings. Today it enrolls about 1,600 students in 38 programs. (All history here is invented for the demonstration.)

Traditions

The Lantern Walk, the first-snow quad snowball truce and the spring Garden Day are favorite rituals shared across generations.

Meet the president

President Dr. Helena Voss (fictional) is a historian of science who has led Alderbrook since 2022. She teaches one first-year seminar every spring.

About this demonstration site

Alderbrook College is fictional. This site is a static front-end replica built for accessibility and usability research; it has no server, collects no data and sends no messages. Forms and portals are simulations. Accessibility feedback on the research dataset should be sent through the project’s repository, not to any address on this site.
