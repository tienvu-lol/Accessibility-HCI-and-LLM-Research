Frequently asked questions

What should first-year applicants prepare?

For this sample, organize your coursework, activities, and academic interests. The preparation demo produces a local checklist only.

How do transfer applicants start?

Gather course descriptions and previous college records, then consider how your interests fit a program. No credit evaluation is performed here.

Can I save an application here?

No. This replica has no account system and saves no entered data. Closing the page ends the demonstration.
