Types of aid

Grants and scholarships

These can support education without ordinary repayment, subject to their conditions. This site awards no funds.

Work opportunities

Some aid packages include eligible student employment. No jobs or work awards are available through this demo.

Loans

Loans generally must be repaid under agreed terms. Review the actual terms carefully with the relevant provider.

Applying for aid

  1. Review the actual requirements for your institution and circumstances.
  2. Gather required records before starting an official application.
  3. Read notices and follow up on any additional documentation.

Managing your aid

Keep track of award terms, enrollment requirements, and renewal dates. Review your budget when your circumstances change.

Financial literacy and understanding costs

Separate billed expenses from personal expenses. A sample budget is a planning exercise rather than a promise of your actual costs.
