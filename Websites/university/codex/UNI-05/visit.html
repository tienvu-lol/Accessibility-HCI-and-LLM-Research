Make the most of your visit

How should I prepare?

Write down questions about classes, community and access. Wear comfortable shoes and check the weather before a real visit.

What should I do during a first visit?

Talk with students, explore a classroom area and give yourself time to reflect on what you notice.

A typical sample route includes the green, a science space and a gathering place. Actual tours may differ.
