Renew a Vehicle Registration Online

Renew your registration in about ten minutes if your registration is current and your insurance is on file. This page is a fictional example.

On this page

  • Before you start
  • How to renew
  • Fees
  • After you renew
  • Help and contact

Before you start

Have these ready:

  • The renewal notice, or your plate number and the last four characters of your vehicle identification number
  • Proof of insurance on file with the Motor Vehicles Bureau
  • A credit or debit card (a card is not charged in this demo)

How to renew

  1. Select Start online and enter your plate number.
  2. Confirm your address and vehicle details.
  3. Pay the fee.
  4. Print or save your receipt. Your new sticker arrives by mail in 5 to 7 business days.

After you renew

When will my sticker arrive?

Usually within 5 to 7 business days. Keep your receipt as proof of renewal until then.

What if my address changed?

Update your address during the renewal steps. The new sticker is mailed to the address you confirm.

What if my registration already expired?

You may need to renew in person. Contact the bureau using the details below.

Help and contact
