Accessibility

Our approach to accessibility for this fictional portal.

Accessibility statement

The State of Aldermere is committed to making its fictional portal usable by everyone. This replica targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a goal; it has not been audited and we make no claim of conformance. Known limitations are listed in the project README.

Privacy Policy

This replica collects no data, sets no cookies and makes no network requests. Text typed into demo forms stays in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page. (On the original site, privacy has its own page; here it is combined with this page.)
