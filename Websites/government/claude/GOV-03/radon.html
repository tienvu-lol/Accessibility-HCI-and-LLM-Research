Radon

Radon is a naturally occurring radioactive gas that can build up in buildings. Testing is the only way to know your level.

What is radon?

Radon forms when uranium in soil and rock breaks down. It can seep into homes through cracks in floors and walls. You cannot see, smell or taste it.

How to test your home

  1. Buy a short-term test kit from a hardware store or a licensed provider.
  2. Place it in the lowest lived-in level, away from drafts and humidity.
  3. Leave it for the stated time, then send it to the lab.
  4. If the result is high, run a second test before deciding on repairs.
