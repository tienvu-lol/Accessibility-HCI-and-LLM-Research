Ground Water and Drinking Water

About half of households draw some water from underground sources. This page explains how that water is protected and tested.

Last updated: 2026-09-18 (fictional sample date)

Public water systems

Systems that serve many people test their water on a set schedule and must share results in an annual report. Ask your supplier for the latest report.

Private wells

Well owners are responsible for testing their own water. Test yearly for bacteria and after any flooding or nearby construction.

Common questions

At least once a year for bacteria, and any time the taste, smell or color changes.

Boiling kills many germs but does not remove metals or chemicals. Follow the advice in your local notice.

Start with your water supplier, then contact the agency or your regional office.
