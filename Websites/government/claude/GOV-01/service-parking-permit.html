How to get a resident parking permit

Sample guidance from the fictional Parking Clerk.

Who can get a permit

Residents who live in a permit zone and register a vehicle at that address can apply. Each household may hold up to three permits (sample rule).

What to bring

  • Photo ID showing your Port Alder address, or a utility bill from the last 30 days.
  • Current vehicle registration with the same address.
  • Proof of insurance.

How to apply

  1. Find your permit zone on the neighborhood list.
  2. Gather the documents above.
  3. Visit the Parking Clerk counter at City Hall, Room 102. Online application is a demo only and is not available here.
  4. Pay the annual fee of $30 (sample amount).
  5. Your permit is mailed within five business days.

Questions

Can I get a visitor pass?

Yes. Visitor passes are issued for up to 14 days per month (sample rule).

What if I move?

Return the old permit and apply again with your new address.

What if my permit is lost?

Replacement permits cost $10 (sample amount).
