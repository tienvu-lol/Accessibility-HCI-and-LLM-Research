## Features

- Skip link, alert banner (expand, dismiss), header with Translate demo dialog, search-type panel, primary nav with two dropdown disclosures (Places, Government) and a mobile Menu button.
- Keyword search + filters over inline data with live result count and empty state: services (16), departments (17, with A-Z buttons), events (10), news (8), public notices (8), neighborhoods (12). Header and hero search route to a list page with `?q=`.
- Per-service "What you need" disclosures; how-to page with steps and FAQ details.
- Council meeting schedule table with per-meeting agenda (intentional addition).
- Forms (newsletter, website feedback, report a problem): labeled fields, error summary with focus, inline errors with `aria-invalid`, clearly labeled demo success.
- Instrumentation: `data-section` / `data-component` on regions and components (see `component-inventory.json`).

## Simulations and intentional additions

- Translate: demo dialog, changes nothing. Videos: dialog says no video exists. Forms: nothing is sent or stored; success text and the `DEMO-nnnnn` reference are simulations.
- Intentional additions (not confirmed on the reference): report-a-problem form, council meetings table, parks/libraries sections on the neighborhoods page, alert dismiss button.
- Omitted: Guides (reference returned 503), Business Support / City Projects / Newsletters nav links, AI "Ask" search mode, social links.

## Known limitations

- List pages render from JS; with JavaScript off they show only a notice (the navigation, forms' labels and other content remain).
- Mega-menu and mobile behavior are modeled from the inventory, not from observed reference behavior.
- No article, event or notice detail pages; only the parking permit has a how-to page. Other services show a disclosure.
- Dates are fixed sample dates in Nov-Dec 2026 and will go stale; all numbers, fees and rules are invented.
