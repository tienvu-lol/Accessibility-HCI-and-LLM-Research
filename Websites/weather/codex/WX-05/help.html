Common questions

Is the 14-day table a real forecast?

No. It uses fixed fictional data to demonstrate an extended table's organization. It cannot support travel, work or other planning.

How do temperature units work?

The unit selector converts displayed sample temperatures with °F = °C × 9/5 + 32. It changes this page only and does not store a preference.

Where do the values and city names come from?

They are authored local examples: four fictional cities, three hourly days and fourteen extended rows. No source operational weather is copied.

Are the forecast sections tabs?

They are links to separate HTML pages, matching the approved page hierarchy. A selected-page indicator identifies the current view.
