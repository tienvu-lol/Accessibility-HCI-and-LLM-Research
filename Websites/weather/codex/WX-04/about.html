About this fictional service

Weather, made understandable.

Northlight Weather is an original research identity, not a meteorological authority.

Our sample approach

This static site explores how a national weather service organizes forecast, warning and map information. Three fictional places and five sample days let evaluators inspect representative data and navigation.

No observations, climate records or operational forecasts are supplied.

What the demonstration covers

Local forecast tables, day navigation, explanatory disclosures, date-based warning examples and a schematic rainfall timeline.
