Cyclone Information

What is a Cyclone?

Tropical cyclones are rotating storm systems characterized by a low-pressure center, strong thunderstorms, and a well-defined circulation.

Safety Tips

  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Prepare emergency supplies.
  • Follow evacuation orders if issued.
