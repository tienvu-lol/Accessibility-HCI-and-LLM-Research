Indigenous weather knowledge

Exploring the deep connection between Indigenous cultures and the weather.

Traditional Knowledge

Indigenous peoples have observed and interpreted weather patterns for thousands of years. This knowledge, passed down through generations, offers valuable insights into seasonal changes, animal behavior, and environmental indicators.

Traditional Seasons

Season 1: Spring

Characterized by blooming flora and specific bird migrations. Indicates the start of warmer temperatures.

Season 2: Summer

Marked by thunderstorms and specific insect activity. A time for hunting and gathering.

Season 3: Autumn

Signaled by falling leaves and changes in wind direction. Preparation for cooler weather.

Season 4: Winter

Defined by cold winds and specific star positions. A time for storytelling and community.
