Guides and glossary

Reading a forecast table

Highs and lows are the warmest and coolest sample temperatures expected for a day. The precipitation chance is an invented probability that some place in the area sees measurable rain or snow. All of it is made up for this research replica.

Watch, warning, advisory: the labels

In this sample scheme, a watch means conditions could develop, a warning means they are expected, an advisory means a lesser but still disruptive event, and a statement is background information. Each label also appears as text, never colour alone.

Winter preparedness checklist (sample)

Keep a fictional kit: flashlight, spare batteries, blankets, water and a battery radio. This list is generic placeholder advice and not official guidance.

Storm season checklist (sample)

Review an invented evacuation route, charge devices, and photograph belongings. Placeholder advice only; follow your real local officials for real events.

Glossary of terms (sample)

Front: the boundary between two air masses. Dew point: a measure of moisture in the air. Reflectivity: how strongly radar echoes return from precipitation. Cone: the area a storm center might travel.
