About the Authority

Meridian Weather Authority is a fictional public meteorological agency created for a research dataset. It does not exist and issues no forecasts.

Our fictional mission

To show how a national weather website is organised: forecasts, alerts, observations, safety guidance and local offices.

How it is organised

Five invented regions, each with forecast offices and sample stations.

About the data

Every number is invented and stored inside the page script. None of it measures anything.
