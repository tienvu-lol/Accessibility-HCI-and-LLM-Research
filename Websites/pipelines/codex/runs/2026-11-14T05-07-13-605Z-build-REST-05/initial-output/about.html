Our approach

A sense of place.

Ember & Elm is an original fictional restaurant group, imagined for a study of web accessibility and usability.

Start with the room

A corner table can change an evening. So can a familiar welcome, the right light, and a conversation that has time to wander.

Our imagined collection gives every room a distinct point of view. The connection is a shared interest in the small details that make people feel at ease.

Then make it personal

The dining rooms in this demonstration are fictional. Their names, city associations, artwork and stories were written for the replica rather than taken from an operating group.
