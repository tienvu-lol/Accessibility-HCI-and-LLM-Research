Shipping & Returns

A useful place to pause before the next project.

This is a fictional help page for the local demo. Circuit Grove does not accept orders, ship items or process returns.

Shipping in this demonstration

The cart shows sample merchandise totals only. There are no carrier quotes, delivery dates, addresses or order tracking. No customer information is collected.

Why is there no shipping estimate?

This catalog runs entirely on your device. It has no connection to a warehouse or shipping service, so it cannot provide a real estimate.

Returns in this demonstration

No purchase occurs, so a return is never needed. To remove a sample item, set its cart quantity to zero and choose Update quantities. Empty cart removes all samples at once.

How can I reset the demo?

Use Empty cart on the cart page. This clears its nonpersonal product selections and sample coupon. If browser storage is blocked, the cart already resets when you navigate.
