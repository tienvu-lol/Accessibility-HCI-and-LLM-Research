Our Story

Founded in 2005 by immigrant brothers who brought the flavors of Xi’an’s street markets to New York City, Xi’an Famous Foods began as a modest stall in Flushing, Queens.

Our menu centers on hand‑pulled noodles, fragrant spice blends, and recipes passed down through generations. Each dish is prepared fresh daily, using traditional techniques such as the “biang‑biang” noodle pull and the slow‑braising of lamb in cumin and chili.

Today we serve guests across multiple locations, staying true to our mission: to share the bold, aromatic tastes of Northwestern China with everyone who walks through our doors.

“We cook the way our grandparents taught us — with patience, bold spices, and a lot of love.”
