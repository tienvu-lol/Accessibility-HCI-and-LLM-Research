Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe?
Visit our Subscribe page to choose a plan and complete the signup process.
I’m having trouble logging in. What should I do?
Try resetting your password using the “Forgot password” link on the login page. If problems persist, contact our support team.
Can I access the site on my mobile device?
Yes. Our site is fully responsive and we also offer iOS and Android apps available in the respective app stores.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Log in to your account, go to Account Settings, and select “Cancel Subscription.” You’ll retain access until the end of your billing period.
Where can I send news tips?
Email news@cascadiatimes.example or use our secure tip form.
