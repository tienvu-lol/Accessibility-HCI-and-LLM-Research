About Lone Star University

A leading public research university dedicated to excellence and innovation.

Our History

Founded in 1883, Lone Star University has grown from a small agricultural college to a major research institution. Our campus spans over 400 acres and serves thousands of students from around the world.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education, conduct groundbreaking research, and serve the community with integrity and compassion.
