About the Aurora Meteorological Bureau

Serving the nation with trusted weather information for over a century.

Our Mission

Mission Statement

To provide accurate, timely, and accessible weather and climate information to protect life and property, and to support the nation's economy and environment.

Our Values

  • Accuracy: We strive for the highest quality data and forecasts.
  • Reliability: We are here when you need us most.
  • Accessibility: We make our information available to everyone.

Our History

Founded in 1901, the Aurora Meteorological Bureau has a long history of serving the nation. From simple barometers to advanced satellite technology, we have always been at the forefront of weather science.
