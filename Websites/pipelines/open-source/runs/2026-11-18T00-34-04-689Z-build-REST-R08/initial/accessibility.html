Accessibility Statement

Jade Dragon Kitchen is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone, and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Measures to support accessibility

  • We include proper heading levels, landmarks, and ARIA labels where needed.
  • All interactive elements are keyboard operable and have visible focus indicators.
  • We provide text alternatives for non‑text content.
  • Color contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines.
  • We respect prefers-reduced-motion and avoid unnecessary animation.
  • Forms include associated labels, inline error messages, and error summary.
