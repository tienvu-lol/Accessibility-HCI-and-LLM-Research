Core Curriculum

Requirements for all undergraduate students at Lone Star University.

General Education Requirements

  • English Composition (6 hours)
  • Mathematics (3-6 hours)
  • Social & Behavioral Sciences (6 hours)
  • Natural Sciences (6-8 hours)
  • Humanities (3 hours)
  • Art (3 hours)
  • U.S. History (3 hours)
  • U.S. Government (3 hours)

Students should consult their academic advisor for specific requirements based on their major.
