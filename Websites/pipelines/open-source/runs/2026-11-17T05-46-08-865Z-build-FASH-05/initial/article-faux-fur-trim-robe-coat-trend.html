The Data Shows It: The Faux Fur-Trimmed Coat Is the Coat of the Season

Why oversized, cozy silhouettes are dominating street style from Milan to New York.

By Elena Rostova

The data is in, and it's clear: the faux fur-trimmed coat is the defining outerwear piece of the season. From the runways of Milan to the streets of New York, designers and street style icons alike are embracing this oversized, cozy silhouette.

Why It's Trending

Sustainability concerns have driven a shift towards faux fur, which offers the look and feel of real fur without the ethical implications. Combined with the growing demand for comfort and warmth, it's no surprise that this trend has taken off.

How to Style It

The key to styling a faux fur-trimmed coat is balance. Pair it with slim-fit jeans and ankle boots for a casual look, or dress it up with a slip dress and heels for a night out.
