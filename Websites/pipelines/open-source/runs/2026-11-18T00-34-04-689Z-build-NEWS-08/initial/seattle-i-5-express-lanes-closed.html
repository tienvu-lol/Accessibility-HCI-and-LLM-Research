Seattle’s I-5 express lanes suddenly closed by severe cracking

By Jordan Lee • November 1, 2026

Seattle’s I-5 express lanes were shut down in both directions early this morning after inspectors discovered severe cracking in the concrete pavement near the Ship Canal Bridge.

The Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) said the damage appeared overnight and poses an immediate safety risk. “We cannot allow traffic to use the lanes until we fully assess the extent of the deterioration,” a WSDOT spokesperson said.

Commuters are being directed to use the general-purpose lanes or alternative routes such as I-90 and State Route 520. Traffic planners expect significant delays during the morning and evening rush hours.

Engineers are conducting non‑destructive testing to determine whether the cracking is limited to the surface or extends deeper into the slab. If the damage is structural, repairs could take several weeks.

The express lanes, which opened in 2015 to ease congestion, have carried an average of 45,000 vehicles per weekday. Their sudden closure highlights the aging infrastructure challenges facing the region.

Local officials urge drivers to plan ahead, allow extra travel time, and consider public transit options where available.
