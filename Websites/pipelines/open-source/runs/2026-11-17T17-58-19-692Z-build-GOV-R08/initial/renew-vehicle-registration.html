Renew your vehicle or trailer registration

Quick steps to renew your registration online or in person.

How to renew

  1. Verify insurance coverage – your policy must be active and match the vehicle details.
  2. Complete safety and emissions inspection (if required) – some vehicles need a current inspection sticker.
  3. Gather title and registration documents – have your current registration and proof of identity ready.
  4. Submit renewal online or in person – use the RMV website or visit a branch office.
  5. Receive new registration and decals – the new registration is mailed or provided instantly at the office.
