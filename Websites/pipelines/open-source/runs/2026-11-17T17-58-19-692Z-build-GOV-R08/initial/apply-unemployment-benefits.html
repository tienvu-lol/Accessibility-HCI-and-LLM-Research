Apply for unemployment insurance benefits

Step‑by‑step guidance for filing an unemployment claim in Massachusetts.

How to apply

  1. Determine eligibility – you must have lost work through no fault of your own and meet wage requirements.
  2. Gather required documents – Social Security number, driver’s license, employment history for the past 18 months.
  3. File initial claim online – use the UI Online portal or call the TeleClaim center.
  4. Complete weekly claim certification – certify each week you are unemployed and able to work.
  5. Attend any required eligibility review – you may be scheduled for a fact‑finding interview.
  6. Receive benefits – payments are made via direct deposit or a debit card.
