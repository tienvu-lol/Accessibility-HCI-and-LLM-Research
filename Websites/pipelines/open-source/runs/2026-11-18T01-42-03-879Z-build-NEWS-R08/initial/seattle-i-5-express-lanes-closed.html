Seattle’s I-5 express lanes suddenly closed by severe cracking

Published November 5, 2026 • 6:00 PM PST

Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) announced the sudden closure of the Interstate 5 express lanes in downtown Seattle after engineers discovered severe cracking in the concrete deck.

The closure affects both northbound and southbound express lanes between the Seneca Street and Mercer Street exits. WSDOT said the damage was discovered during a routine inspection and poses a safety risk to vehicles traveling at high speeds.

“We are taking a precautionary approach,” said WSDOT spokesperson Lena Ortiz. “The express lanes will remain closed until we can complete a full structural assessment and determine the appropriate repair strategy.”

Regular lanes on I-5 remain open, but traffic is expected to increase significantly during the morning and evening commutes. WSDOT encourages drivers to consider alternate routes such as State Route 99 or public transit options.

The agency has set up a detour signage plan and will provide real‑time updates via its website and social media channels. Estimated reopening timeline is not yet available, as engineers need to evaluate whether the cracking is isolated or indicative of a broader issue.

Local businesses near the express lanes have expressed concern about the impact on deliveries and customer access. The city of Seattle is coordinating with WSDOT to mitigate disruptions.
