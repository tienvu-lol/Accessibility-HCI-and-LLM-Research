Most locations are open from 6am to 9pm, Monday through Sunday. Hours may vary by location.

How do I check my gift card balance?

You can check your balance on our Gift Cards page or at any Harvest Table location.

Do you provide allergen information?

Yes, allergen information is available on our menu and upon request at any location.

Do you offer catering?

Yes, we offer catering for events of all sizes. Please contact your local location for details.
