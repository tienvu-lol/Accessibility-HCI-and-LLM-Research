## Approval basis

Verbatim instruction from the project lead in the orchestrator session on 2026-11-13, given
immediately after the orchestrator reported that the Claude group had 30 verified sources with
zero recorded approvals and that Claude builds could not legitimately start until approval was
recorded:

> begin the build phase, do 3 at once

This is recorded as a blanket Phase A approval over the Claude group's own slots, consistent with
`Rules.md` §3 as amended ("Approval is per model group over its own slots") and
`MODEL_ALLOCATION.md` §2 "Active allocation".

## Scope and limits

- Covers **Claude group only** (slots 01–03). It grants nothing to the Codex group (already
  approved 2026-11-13 under its own record) and nothing to the open-source group (slots 07–10,
  still `planned`, no sources researched).
- Covers only rows whose `verification_result` was already `verified` at the time of recording.
  All 30 qualified; no row was approved on an unverified or UNVERIFIED source.
- `PORT-01`–`PORT-03` are **not** covered: they were approved 2026-11-09 and already built. Their
  existing `approved_at` values were left untouched.
- Per `Rules.md` §3, this approval does not relax any other gate. A source that is later found
  changed, dead or substituted still stops the build for that slot and requires a fresh approval
  for the replacement.
