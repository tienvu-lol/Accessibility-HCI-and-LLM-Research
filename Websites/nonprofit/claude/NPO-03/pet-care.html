Pet care

General care guidance for dogs, cats and horses. This is sample content, not veterinary advice.

Care guides

Dog care basics

  • Daily exercise suited to age and breed
  • Fresh water and measured meals
  • Yearly check-ups and parasite prevention
  • Gentle, reward-based training

Cat care basics

  • A clean litter box for each cat, plus one extra
  • Scratching posts and vertical space
  • Indoor play every day
  • Regular dental and wellness checks

Horse care basics

  • Constant access to clean water
  • Forage-first diet adjusted to workload
  • Hoof care every six to eight weeks
  • Shelter from heat, cold and wind
