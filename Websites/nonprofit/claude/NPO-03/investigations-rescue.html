Investigations and rescue

Our field team investigates cruelty, supports prosecutors and rescues animals in danger.

What we do

  • Cruelty investigations in cooperation with local law enforcement
  • Large-scale rescues, including hoarding and fighting cases
  • Disaster response and emergency pet sheltering

How it works

  1. A report arrives from the public or a partner agency.
  2. Investigators assess the situation and coordinate with authorities.
  3. Rescue teams remove animals safely when authorized.
  4. Animals receive care, and evidence supports legal action.
