About us

Our mission is to prevent cruelty to animals and to make kindness the everyday standard.

Our mission

Hollowbrook Animal Alliance was founded by neighbors who believed every animal should have food, safety and care. Today we run rescue teams, clinics, adoption centers and advocacy campaigns across five regions.

This site, its people and its figures are invented for a research dataset.

Our impact (sample figures)

  • 39,900animals helped this year
  • 3,130adoptions
  • 14partner shelters
  • 9,300clinic visits

History

  1. 1998: A volunteer foster network begins in Port Larkin.
  2. 2007: The first low-cost clinic opens.
  3. 2015: Investigations and rescue team formed.
  4. 2023: Grants program for partner shelters launches.
