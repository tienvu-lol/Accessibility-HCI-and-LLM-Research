Helping shelters, people and pets

We partner with shelters and families so more animals stay safe and more pets stay home.

How we help

  • Spay and neuter access

    Mobile clinics and vouchers reduce unwanted litters.

  • Pet-food pantries

    Families in need receive food so pets are not surrendered.

  • Shelter partnerships

    Training and equipment for animal-control staff.
