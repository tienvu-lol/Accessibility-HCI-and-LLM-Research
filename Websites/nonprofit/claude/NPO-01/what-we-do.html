What we do

Four programme areas, one goal: a safe, healthy and learning childhood for every child.

  • Learning
  • Health and nutrition
  • Child protection
  • Safe water and climate
  • Emergency response

Learning

We help children start school on time, stay in school and learn to read.

  • Training for teachers and school leaders
  • Catch-up classes and reading circles
  • Flood-ready and solar-powered classrooms

Health and nutrition

Healthy first years shape a lifetime.

  • Routine vaccination and mobile clinics
  • Screening and treatment for malnutrition
  • Fortified food partnerships with local mills

Child protection

Children need to be safe at home, at school and online.

  • Peer-led safe spaces for teenagers
  • Support for families under stress
  • Training for youth workers and teachers

Safe water and climate

Clean water keeps children healthy and in class.

  • Solar pumps and handwashing stations at schools
  • Drought-ready water systems
  • Community repair funds and caretaker training

Emergency response

When emergencies strike we deliver water, shelter, food and safe places for children to learn and play.
