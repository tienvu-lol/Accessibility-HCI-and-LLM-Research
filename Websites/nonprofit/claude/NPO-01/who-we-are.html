Who we are

Harborlight Children's Fund works so that every child can grow up safe, healthy and learning.

  • Mission
  • Governance
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Safeguarding

Our mission

We believe childhood should be a safe harbor. We partner with families, schools, health workers and local governments to reach the children who are hardest to reach, and we respond fast when disaster strikes.

  • Children first. Every decision is tested against a child's best interests.
  • Local leadership. We work through community partners and invest in local staff.
  • Evidence. We measure results and publish what we learn.

Governance

An independent board of trustees oversees strategy and finances. A youth advisory panel of fifteen members meets quarterly and reports directly to the board. Board members and panel names in this demo are not listed because the organisation is fictional.

Safeguarding

Everyone who works with us signs a code of conduct, completes safeguarding training and can report concerns confidentially. We investigate every report and share outcomes with the board. See the FAQ for how to raise a concern.
