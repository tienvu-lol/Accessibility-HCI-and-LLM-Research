Donate food

Healthy, unopened food keeps our shelves useful. Here is what helps most.

Most needed

  • Canned beans, vegetables and fruit in juice
  • Peanut butter and other nut or seed butters
  • Whole grain pasta, rice and oats
  • Low-sodium soups and tuna or chicken pouches
  • Shelf-stable milk and baby formula

Please skip

  • Anything opened, expired or damaged
  • Homemade or home-canned items
  • Glass jars when a can or pouch is available
  • Alcohol
