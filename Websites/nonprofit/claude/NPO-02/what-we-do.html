What we do

Good food moves fastest when many hands carry it. Here is how our work fits together.

Our programs

    Warehouse to pantry, mobile market and meal site, five days a week.

Food+ Partnerships

Clinics, shelters and tenant groups host a food shelf next to the services people already use. Partners share intake forms so a family tells their story once.

Meet our partners

Advocacy & Public Policy

We bring neighbors’ experience to county councils and the state house, backing free school meals, simpler benefit applications and fair grocery access.

Ask about advocacy

Nutrition & Wellness

Cooking classes, recipe cards in six languages, and produce prescriptions that clinics can write for patients with diet-related conditions.

See upcoming classes

Farms & Gardening

We buy from small regional farms, support forty community garden plots, and run a seedling swap each spring.
