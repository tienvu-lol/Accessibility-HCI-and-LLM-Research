Direct food distribution

Five ways good food reaches people. Open a program to see who it serves and where to start.

Distribution programs

  • 186 sample pantries pick up fresh and shelf-stable food from our warehouse each week. Most are choice-style, so shoppers pick what their family eats.

    Find a pantry

  • A refrigerated truck sets up like a farmers market in neighborhoods with limited grocery access. No sign-up needed.

    See mobile market stops

  • A monthly box of staples and produce for adults 60 and older, picked up at partner senior centers.

  • Discreet backpacks of easy-to-open food go home on Fridays from partner schools.

  • Student pantries stocked by us, with swipe-free access for anyone who needs it.
