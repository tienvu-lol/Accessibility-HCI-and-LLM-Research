Fieldwork / Trail journal

Leave room for the unexpected.

Role
Design & frontend
Year
2025
Focus
Creative coding

The brief

An offline-inspired journal concept with generous maps, field notes, and a carefully paced reading experience.

Leave room for the unexpected. The goal was to help people complete the next useful step without asking them to understand the entire system first.

From uncertainty to a working pattern

01 / Understand

Map the primary journey and write the smallest possible set of requirements. Include empty states, long labels, and keyboard-only navigation from the beginning.

02 / Explore

Compare a compact list with an image-led layout. Use hierarchy and spacing to make the most important information easy to scan.

03 / Refine

Test the prototype at narrow widths, zoom the text, and check focus order. Keep the version that needs the fewest explanations.

Interface studies

Select a placeholder study to open the gallery.

What changed

The final concept uses fewer competing actions, clearer labels, and a predictable reading order. This is a fictional design exploration; no user-study results or commercial outcomes are claimed.

What would I test next?

Whether people can identify the next step without help, recover from a mistake, and complete the flow using a keyboard and a screen reader.
