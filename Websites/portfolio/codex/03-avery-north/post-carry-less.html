part 0

Leave a little room

A practical note on leaving enough room to pay attention. A clear plan is useful, but it should leave enough space to notice when the story is somewhere else. Begin with a sense of direction rather than a fixed list of pictures.

part 1

Look beyond the obvious frame

The wider view explains where you are. A smaller detail can explain how it feels. Move between the two and pay attention to the images that connect them.

part 2

Make a sequence, not a pile

Give each frame a reason to be there. A shift in scale, tone, or pace can make the next image more meaningful. Removing a good picture can sometimes make a stronger story.

part 3

Keep a note for next time

Write down what the conditions taught you, what you carried without using, and what you wish you had noticed earlier. A small observation is a useful starting point for the next journey.
