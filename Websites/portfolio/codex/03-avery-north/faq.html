Questions / answers

A few things to know.

Useful context before you explore the studio.

Can I book a real commission here?

No. Avery North is fictional. The inquiry and booking forms demonstrate frontend interactions only.

Are these photographs?

No. They are original landscape illustrations used as local placeholder assets, not copied work or photographs by Alex Strohl.

What is included in a print order?

Only a local success state. There is no actual order, payment, delivery, or license agreement.

How does image licensing work in this demo?

Choose Image licensing in the inquiry form and describe an imaginary use. No legal license is issued.

Why is one project password gated?

The Outside hours case study includes an insecure demonstration gate. Enter openstudio; its content is still present in the source.

How is availability determined?

The availability label is fictional and static. The local-time badge uses the browser’s America/Denver time-zone data.
