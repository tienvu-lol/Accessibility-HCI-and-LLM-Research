Motion that earns its place

A small set of rules for animation that explains rather than distracts.

2025 · Engineering · 3 min read · Fictional editorial content

On this pageBegin with a useful questionMake the next step legibleCheck the awkward statesLeave a clear trail

Begin with a useful question

A small set of rules for animation that explains rather than distracts. Before changing a screen, write down what someone is trying to do. A clear task gives every visual choice a purpose.

Make the next step legible

Group related information, use descriptive labels, and let spacing explain relationships. A person should not have to study a whole page to understand where to begin.

Check the awkward states

Try an empty list, a very long title, a slow connection, and a missed required field. The edges tell you whether the underlying pattern is strong.

Leave a clear trail

Document the decisions that future contributors will otherwise need to guess. Small, specific notes are easier to maintain than a large set of abstract rules.
