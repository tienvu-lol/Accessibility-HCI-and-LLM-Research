About Rowan

I’m Rowan, a fictional frontend engineer who cares about how an interface feels to use, how clearly it communicates, and how well it holds up over time.

My favorite work brings order to complexity: a library catalogue that invites exploration, a service form that gets out of the way, or a component system that helps a small team move with confidence.

Outside the screen, I keep a field notebook, walk unfamiliar streets, and collect books with very good margins.

How I work

I start with the task someone needs to complete, map the awkward edges, and make an unstyled prototype. Visual polish comes after the flow is understandable.

I value short feedback loops, plain documentation, and making room for people who use the web differently.
