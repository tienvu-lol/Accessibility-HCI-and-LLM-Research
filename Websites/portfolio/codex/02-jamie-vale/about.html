about.

I like making complicated things feel a little more human.

A designer who likes to build.

I’m Jamie, a fictional independent product designer and frontend developer based in Sydney. I work where visual thinking meets practical implementation.

I’m happiest when I can follow an idea from a rough sketch to a working interface. That means asking the awkward questions early, keeping the important things visible, and testing with more than a mouse.

Outside work, I draw little maps, cook without quite following the recipe, and keep an unreasonable number of notebooks.
