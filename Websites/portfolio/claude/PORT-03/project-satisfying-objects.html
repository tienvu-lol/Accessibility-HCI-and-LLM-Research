Satisfying Objects vol. 6

Six objects, six loops, one rule: the cut has to be invisible at quarter speed.

Project facts
ClientPersonal work
Duration6 loops, 4–6 seconds each
ToolsBlender, Redshift
DeliverySquare and vertical, 4K
StatusPersonal work, 2024

The brief

Six objects, six loops, one rule: the cut has to be invisible at quarter speed.

The problem

A series has to feel like a series without each piece repeating the last. The constraint — invisible at quarter speed — did that work, but it rules out most of the easy tricks, including the one where you hide the join behind motion blur.

How it was made

  • Pick objects with different weights: rubber, brass, glass, sand, paper, water.
  • Give each one the same loop length tolerance and let the material decide the timing.
  • Review every loop at 25% speed before anything is graded.
  • Publish them a week apart, so the series reads as a series rather than a dump.

Where it got to

  • Six pieces, five weeks, one abandoned (the water loop never closed convincingly).
  • The series brought in two of the studio's four 2025 client projects.
  • The sand loop is still the most requested as a print, which I do not sell.
“A constraint stated in one sentence will shape a series better than a mood board.”
