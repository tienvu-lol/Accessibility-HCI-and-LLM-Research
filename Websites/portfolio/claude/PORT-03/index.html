About the studio

One person, one workstation, a render box in the hall cupboard that the neighbours can hear in February. I model, light, animate and grade everything myself, which keeps the work consistent and the studio small on purpose.

Most pieces start on paper, as a timing diagram rather than a storyboard: where the weight falls, where the eye should be, where the loop closes. The render is the last and least interesting part.
