Terminal 4 Arrivals

A 90-metre arrivals wall that reads as one continuous loop across eleven screens.

Project facts
ClientArlanda Airport
DurationContinuous loop, 11 screens
ToolsBlender, TouchDesigner, Notch
Delivery11 synced 4K feeds, unattended
StatusInstallation, 2025

The brief

A 90-metre arrivals wall that reads as one continuous piece.

The problem

Eleven screens, 400 millimetres of bezel between each, and an audience walking past at four kilometres an hour. Anything that depends on a viewer standing still fails, and anything that loops in under a minute becomes visible wallpaper by week two.

How it was made

  • Design for the walk, not the stand: the motion travels at roughly walking pace in the opposite direction.
  • Treat the bezels as part of the composition rather than something to hide.
  • A 22-minute loop so that nobody passing twice a day sees the same moment.
  • Ship a one-page maintenance note with a photograph of every cable.

Where it got to

  • Running unattended since October 2025, with one restart in the first month.
  • Twenty-two minutes of material from eleven weeks of work.
  • The maintenance note has been used twice by people who have never met me.
“Installation work is 60% animation and 40% making sure a stranger can restart it on a Sunday.”
