How a project runs

  1. Brief and call. Twenty minutes, no deck. If it is not a fit I will say so and suggest someone else.
  2. Timing diagram. Before anything is modelled, a one-page diagram of where the weight falls and where the eye should be.
  3. Blockout. Grey, unlit, correct timing. This is the round where changes are cheap, so this is the round to be difficult in.
  4. Look development. Materials and lighting on one frame, approved as a still.
  5. Render and grade. Half-speed renders, retimed, then graded for the screen it will live on.
  6. Handover. Files, crops, and for installations a maintenance note with photographs.
