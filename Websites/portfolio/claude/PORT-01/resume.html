Fifteen years of front-end and design engineering. The short version is on the about page; this is the one with dates.

Summary

Design engineer who works at the seam between design systems and the browser. I am the person you call when 240 screens each solve the same problem differently, or when a prototype has to be real enough to test with people who use a keyboard and a screen reader.

Experience

  1. 2023–now

    Principal interaction engineer

    Harborlight · Portland, Oregon

    • Own the component library used by six product teams: 74 components, one keyboard model, one focus specification.
    • Cut median time-to-first-interaction on the scheduling view from 3.1s to 0.9s by deleting code rather than adding it.
    • Ran the accessibility programme: 2.2 AA baseline on every new screen, with automated checks in review and quarterly manual passes.
    • Mentor four engineers; run a fortnightly “break the interface” session that has become the team’s favourite meeting.
  2. 2019–2023

    Senior front-end engineer

    Marrow & Pike · Remote

    • Rebuilt the newsroom editor used by 400 staff: any action reachable in three keystrokes, and no action that needed a mouse.
    • Took the public site from 1.9 MB to 240 KB per page, which halved bounce on mobile connections.
    • Wrote the editorial component documentation, which is still the onboarding path for new staff.
  3. 2015–2019

    Interactive developer

    Ferrous Studio · Seattle

    • Twenty-odd campaign microsites and four physical installations, shipped in crates with their own QA plan.
    • Introduced the studio’s first accessibility checklist after an installation failed in front of a client.
  4. 2011–2015

    Front-end developer

    Rook Street Digital · Melbourne

    • Agency work across 40+ sites. Learned semantics, review discipline and how to read a design file.
