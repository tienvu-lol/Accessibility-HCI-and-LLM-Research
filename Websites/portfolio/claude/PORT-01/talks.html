Inviting me to speak

I take three or four engagements a year and I like small rooms. Workshops need three hours and a table; talks need thirty to forty-five minutes and a lectern I can reach the keyboard from.

  • Travel from Portland, Oregon, Pacific time. Happy to do remote.
  • No fee for community events, student groups or anything free to attend.
  • Captions and a text transcript, or I would rather not do it.
