About Ridgeway News

A wider perspective begins with listening close to home.

Our mission

Ridgeway is an imagined nonprofit newsroom serving Southwest and Southside Virginia. This demonstration explores how regional journalism can help readers understand public decisions and connect stories across county lines.

We organize reporting by place and topic. Our fictional stories focus on housing, education, public spaces and the work that holds communities together.

Accountability and annual reports

An independent newsroom should explain how it works and how it is supported. In this sample annual overview, editorial priorities include readable public information, regional context and clear corrections.

This research demonstration has no operating budget, donors, employees or measured outcomes. There is no real annual report to download.

How we approach stories

We aim to distinguish facts from opinions, explain uncertainty and make space for questions. All names and reporting on these pages are fictional and should not be treated as public information.
