Is access free?

All pages in this fictional newsroom are freely available. There is no paywall, paid subscription or login.

How is the newsroom funded?

The source-inspired nonprofit model relies on reader support and other funding while keeping journalism freely available. Ridgeway itself is only a research demonstration: it accepts no money and has no real members or donors.

The support link brings you here to this explanation. No payment process is offered.

How do newsletters work?

The local form lets you select newsletters and try validation. Its success message confirms a demonstration only. No address leaves your device or is stored.

Try the newsletter form

How are corrections handled?

A real newsroom should identify material corrections clearly. This demo contains invented stories and has no staffed contact channel. Learn more about the project on the about page.
