## Approximated

HTTP headings and controls establish hierarchy, not visual geometry. Newspaper typography, teal accents, grid ratios, images, spacing, responsive disclosure and simplified footer are original approximations; no rendered reference was observed. ## Simulated

Newsletter selection, validation and completion are local inferred behavior. Success clearly says no subscription, email or persistence. Mobile menu behavior is an inferred accessible approximation.

## Intentionally added

Protocol-required 404, visible fictional-publication notices on all pages, notice adjacent to article headline, descriptive art alternatives, accessible skip link and error/status feedback. No search-results page or panel added: source research expressly excluded this without further approval.

## Policy/content substitutions

Original low-stakes reading-room feature replaces the observed political article’s subject while preserving editorial template hierarchy. All people/places/quotes invented. Domestic labels Community, Craft and Discovery replace political, crime/legal and science/health headings; no elections, crime, public-health advice, emergencies, obituaries, real officials or fabricated current-event reporting. No breaking-news or emergency banners, live data or playable fabricated broadcast. This topical divergence is deliberate, not an observed source difference.

## Scope omissions

Unmapped topic fronts use homepage anchors; subscriptions, sign-in, external directory services, uninspected policies and real media omitted. No latest/most-read tabs were observed, so none invented. Source alerts/offers newsletter grouping is represented by occasional publication notices rather than an alert channel.

Exact source imagery, layout and metadata beyond recorded headings were not copied.
