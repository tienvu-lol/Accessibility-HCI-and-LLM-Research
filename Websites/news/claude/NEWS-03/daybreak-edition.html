Daybreak Edition

The weekday morning news program from Larkspur Public Media. Sample program page with invented hosts and episodes.

About the program

Daybreak Edition starts each weekday with the news that will matter by lunchtime: a short run of headlines, one long conversation, and a closing story chosen for its sound. Hosts in three time zones hand off to each other in real time.

Hosts: Mei-Lin Adair, Rufus Okonkwo-Hale, Saskia Brandt.
