About Quillon Valley Public Media

Who we are

Quillon Valley Public Media (QVPM) is an invented non-profit broadcaster in the fictional Republic of Veloria. It was created as sample content so researchers can study navigation, forms and reading patterns on a public-media style site.

It has no staff, no listeners, no stations on air and no budget. Every programme and call sign on this site is made up.

Standards of invention

Stories here are deliberately gentle and evergreen. They avoid real people, organisations, events and places, and avoid subjects where invented reporting could mislead.

Accessibility

The site is built toward WCAG 2.2 AA as a goal. That is a target, not a claim of conformance. Report problems to the invented address access@qvpm.example or call 555-0142 (not a real line).
