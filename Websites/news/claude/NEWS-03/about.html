About Larkspur Public Media

Everything here is fictional sample copy.

About, policies and contact

Larkspur Public Media is a fictional non-commercial broadcaster used to study how people use news websites. It has a pretend network of four stations, one newsroom and a made-up history.

The fictional newsroom commits to accuracy, fairness, independence and transparency. This sample text is original and does not reproduce any real organization’s code.

This replica is built to a WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented baseline for research. It has not been audited, and no claim of conformance is made. See README.md in the project folder for what was and was not tested.

Sample figures only: 41% listener gifts, 27% sponsors, 20% grants, 12% other. These numbers are invented.

Press inquiries would go to a media desk. In this replica there is no inbox: nothing you type anywhere here is sent.

A real broadcaster publishes corrections and a public editor’s notes. Here, this entry only marks where they would live.

This site sets no cookies, loads no trackers, and makes no network requests. The choice stored by the station picker stays in your browser.

There are no open roles and no sponsorship packages. Both are placeholders for the reference’s Get Involved links.
