Pellwick opens a pedestrian bridge with a fountain of lanterns

The Candlewharf Footbridge links two invented neighbourhoods and carries more bicycles than anyone planned for.

By Perrin Aldoway (invented reporter)

Sample date: 2099-03-14 · Fictional dateline, Republic of Veloria

Share (non-functional demo)

PELLWICK, Ardenmere (fictional) - On a mild sample morning, the Candlewharf Footbridge opened to walkers, cyclists and one very confident goose. The span crosses the slow Marrow River and joins the Tannery Row and Lower Orchard districts, two neighbourhoods that until now were a twenty-minute detour apart.

What changed for commuters

"I timed it twice," said Orrin Fenwick-Dale, a fictional baker who crosses daily. "Four minutes, and I get to wave at the ferry." The fictional city council says the bridge was built in sections and lifted into place over a single invented weekend.

The deck carries two lanes for bicycles and a wide lane for pedestrians, with benches set into the railings. Lanterns along the rail switch on at dusk. A small plaque lists the names of the fictional schoolchildren who voted on the lantern colour: amber won.

What comes next

Planners in this invented city say they will count crossings for a season before deciding whether to add a second bridge downstream. A sample open house is planned for the following Saturday.
