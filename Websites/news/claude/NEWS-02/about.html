About this fictional site

The Alderny Daily Lantern is a fictional newspaper created for an accessibility and usability research dataset. It is modeled on the page structure of a metropolitan daily, but its city (Port Alderny), county (Tessaly County), state (Calvern), suburbs, institutions, people and stories are all invented. No real events are described.

Sample content policy

The stories are deliberately low-stakes and evergreen. The site contains no elections, crime, courts, emergencies, health guidance, obituaries, weather, market or score data.

Accessibility statement (sample)

The site aims for a WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented baseline. It has not been audited and no conformance claim is made.
