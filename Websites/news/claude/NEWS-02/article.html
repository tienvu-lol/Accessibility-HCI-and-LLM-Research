Fairmeadow Saturday market will settle into the old depot lot

Stallholders say the wider aisles and a shaded bench row are worth the short move.

By Odalys Brenwick (invented byline) | Fairmeadow (fictional dateline) | Sample date: Mar 14, 2031

FAIRMEADOW (fictional) - The Fairmeadow Saturday Market will trade its narrow side street for the gravel-and-grass depot lot beginning with the spring opening, organizers said at a relaxed planning meeting on sample day 14.

"We spent years squeezing strollers past the honey table," said market coordinator Perpetua Halloway-Nix, an invented person. "The depot lot gives every vendor a full tent width and gives shoppers somewhere to sit."

The move adds a covered bench row, a bicycle rack for about forty bikes, and a chalkboard where growers post what is ripe that week. Organizers plan a ribbon of bunting, not a ribbon-cutting, to mark the first Saturday.
