Pennyfold library reopens its reading room after a long renovation

The fictional town library has new shelving, a quieter children’s corner and a reading room that finally keeps the afternoon sun off the page.

By Odalys Fenwright (invented staff writer; not a real person)

HALBROOK CROSS, Orravia (fictional place) · Sample date 20 Bloomtide, Year 7

The reading room of the Pennyfold Free Library reopened on Sample date 20 Bloomtide after eleven months of work on its floor, windows and lighting. Staff said the building’s oak tables were cleaned, not replaced.

What changed

Shelving along the east wall is now low enough for a seated reader to reach. The children’s corner has moved behind a glass partition, and the radiator that rattled for forty years has been swapped for a silent one.

The head librarian, Ansel Brightwater, said the most requested change was reading lamps with warm bulbs. “We ordered forty,” he said. “We could probably have used fifty.”

The library will keep its usual hours. A small exhibition of invented local postcards will sit in the foyer until the end of the month.
