Public Guided Tours

Trained docents lead small groups through the nave, chapels and cloister.

Tours on offer

  • Highlights Tour

    Sixty minutes through the nave, choir and chapels. Included with admission.

  • Light and Glass Tour

    Seventy-five minutes on the stained glass and the rose window. Included with admission.

  • Spires and Stone Tour

    Ninety minutes with a mason, including a tower walk. Extra fee in the real cathedral; fictional here.
