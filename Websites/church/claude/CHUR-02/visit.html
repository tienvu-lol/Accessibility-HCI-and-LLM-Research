Getting here

1 Chapter Close, Harrowmere (fictional address).

  • Bus routes 4 and 9 stop at Chapter Close.
  • No visitor parking; two accessible bays at the south gate.
  • Bicycle racks by the masons’ yard.

Accessibility on site

Getting around

  • Step-free entry at the south gate; level floor through the nave.
  • Large-print guides and a tactile model of the cathedral are available at the Welcome Desk.
  • An induction loop serves the nave and Lady Chapel.
  • Quiet visits can be arranged outside peak hours.
  • Assistance dogs are welcome.

This replica website is a research baseline; it has not been audited, and no claim of accessibility conformance is made.

Digital guide

A pocket audio guide is planned for the real cathedral; this demo does not include one.
