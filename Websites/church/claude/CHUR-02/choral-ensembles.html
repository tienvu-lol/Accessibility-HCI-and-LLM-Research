Professional, community and youth ensembles with rehearsal times.

Ensembles

  • Aurelian Choir of Men and Boys

    Sings Evensong on Thursdays and the Sunday Eucharist. Rehearses Wednesday 4:00 pm. Admission by audition, ages 7 and up.

  • Aurelian Consort

    Eight salaried singers for the Eucharist and chamber concerts. Rehearses Tuesday 6:30 pm.

  • Aurelian Singers

    An open-audition community choir of 80 voices that performs two concerts a year. Rehearses Monday 7:00 pm.

  • Handbell Ringers

    Seasonal handbell music for beginners. Rehearses Saturday 10:00 am.

  • Youth Chorale

    Ages 11 to 18, mixed voices, with a summer tour. Rehearses Thursday 3:30 pm.

  • Little Voices

    Singing games for ages 4 to 7. Saturday 9:30 am.

Audition requests are not part of this demo; they are handled by email in the (fictional) music office: music@aurelian.example.
