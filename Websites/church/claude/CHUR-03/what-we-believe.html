What We Believe

Our mission, the values we try to practice, and the foundations underneath them.

Mission and Vision

We exist to love God, to love our neighbors, and to make a home in Millstone Cove where all are known by name.

Values

  • Common worship

    Praying together from a shared book, with sacraments at the center of the week.

  • Hospitality

    A family-like atmosphere that welcomes new faces and honors differing views.

  • Service

    Taking part in outreach such as the food pantry shelf and the winter coat drive.

  • Lifelong learning

    Exploring scripture, tradition and reason, and the quiet work of the inner life.

Foundations

The Bible is read aloud at every service and studied in small groups.

We value the ancient prayers, hymns and calendar that connect us to Christians across centuries.

We trust that honest questions and careful thought are gifts, not threats, to faith.
