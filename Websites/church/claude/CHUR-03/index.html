Welcome to Saint Aldric’s in Millstone Cove, RI!

We are a congregation of about one hundred and twenty people who gather every week to pray, sing, eat together and look after our neighbors. Newcomers are greeted at the door, shown where the coffee is, and never asked to stand up and introduce themselves.

If you are not sure what to expect on a first visit, the directions and parking notes are a good place to start.

Faith in Action: Worship, Service, and Community for All

Rooted in Tradition

Our worship follows an ancient pattern of readings, prayers, music and shared bread and wine, spoken in plain modern English.

Open to All

Whoever you are and wherever you are on your spiritual path, there is a seat for you. Children, teenagers and elders worship side by side.
