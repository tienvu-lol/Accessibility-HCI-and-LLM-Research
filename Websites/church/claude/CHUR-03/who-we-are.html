Who We Are

A small parish with a big table: worship, hospitality and service shared by everyone who walks in.

Our space

Our hall and chapel are available for community use: birthday parties, anniversaries, concerts, music rehearsals, public forums, and dance or yoga classes. Ask the office about dates.

We are all called to be ministers

Every baptized person is a minister. These are the ideas that shape how we live together. Select a heading to read more.

We aim to be a welcoming household of faith where people encounter God, one another and the wider community of Millstone Cove.

Weekly worship draws on a prayer book tradition: scripture, song, silence and a shared meal at the altar table.

Everyone is invited to take part, including people who are new, doubtful, grieving or just curious.

We stock a food pantry shelf, run a thrift shop for local causes and host recovery and support meetings in our hall.

Adult study, an inquirers’ class and Sunday school help people of every age keep learning. See Adult Formation.

Coffee hour, seasonal fairs and shared meals keep us connected between services.
