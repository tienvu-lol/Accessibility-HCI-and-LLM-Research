Rector

Meet the priest who leads Saint Aldric’s in worship and pastoral care.

The Rev. Marisol Quinn

Marisol serves as rector, preaching most Sundays, visiting members at home and in hospital, and sitting on a number of town committees. Before ordination, Marisol worked as a harbor pilot’s dispatcher and still enjoys a good tide table.

Office conversations are by appointment. Write to the office to book a time.

How the parish called its rector

The vestry formed a search committee, held listening sessions with members, and posted the profile of the parish for candidates. The process took fourteen months and ended with a unanimous vote.
