History

A short, invented timeline of the parish from its founding on the harbor to the present day.

Timeline

  1. 1874: a chapel by the water

    Fishing families raise a small stone chapel and hold the first evening prayer.

  2. 1921: the bell tower

    A bell, cast by subscription, is hung in the new tower and rung for the first time on Easter morning.

  3. 1968: the parish hall

    Volunteers build a hall for Sunday school, suppers and town meetings.

  4. 1998: a ramp and a lift

    The building is made step-free from street to altar.

  5. 2020: worship online

    The congregation learns to pray together through a screen and welcomes neighbors from far away.

  6. Today

    A calm, curious and busy community of about one hundred and twenty members.
