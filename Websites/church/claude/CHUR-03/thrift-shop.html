Trinity Trader Thrift Shop

Gently used clothing, books and housewares, run by volunteers for local causes.

Supporting Our Mission

Every dollar from the shop goes to the parish outreach budget: the food pantry shelf, the winter coat drive and emergency help for neighbors.

Donating goods

We gladly accept

  • Clean clothing and shoes
  • Books and records
  • Kitchenware and linens
  • Small furniture in good repair

We cannot accept

  • Mattresses and large appliances
  • Car seats and cribs
  • Broken or recalled items
