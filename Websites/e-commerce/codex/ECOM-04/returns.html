Returns & exchanges

This is fictional guidance for a local demo. It does not describe the source retailer’s policy.

Your demo questions, answered

Can I return a demo shoe?

No physical items are sold or sent. To undo a sample selection, open your demo bag and choose Remove.

Can I exchange a size?

Remove the old sample from your bag, then choose another size on the Grove Runner page.

What information is saved?

Only fictional product identifiers, sizes and quantities are stored locally when your browser allows it. No contact details are collected. Remove every item to clear your bag.

Where does the demo end?

At the bag. There is no transaction service, mailing form, return label or customer account.
