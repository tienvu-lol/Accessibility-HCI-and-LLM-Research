Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5-8 business days and express takes 2-3 business days. Both are demo estimates.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free when your bag total after discounts is $500 or more. Otherwise standard delivery costs $29.

How do I use a promo code?

Enter the demo code FAMILY10 in your bag. It takes 10% off the subtotal.

Can I change my bag?

Yes. Open the bag to change quantities or remove items. Your bag is saved in this browser only.

What if my item arrives damaged?

In a real store you would contact support with photos. In this demo, use the contact form to see how that flow works.

Do you offer assembly?

Assembly is not part of this demo. Product pages list the dimensions you would need to plan.

Which payment methods can I use?

None. Checkout is a simulation and accepts any well-formed test card number without charging it.

Is this a real store?

No. Lindora Home is a fictional site made for an accessibility and usability study.
