Frequently asked questions

  • In this demo policy, standard orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days and express orders in 1 to 2. Nothing is actually shipped.

  • Standard shipping is free on merchandise totals of $99 or more after discounts. Below that it is $8.95.

  • Orders in this demo cannot be changed because none are placed. A real store would let you edit an order until it is packed.

  • Open the size and fit guide for apparel and wader charts, and compare with a garment that fits you well.

  • Use the returns page to read the policy and fill in the demo return form.

  • Enter the code in the cart. The demo accepts TROUT10 for 10 percent off merchandise.

  • Small pinhole leaks can usually be patched at home with a wader repair kit. Larger tears are best left to a repair shop.

  • Not in this demo. A real retailer would list countries and duties here.
