Larkwater Outfitters is a fictional store created for an accessibility and usability research dataset. Names, prices and policies are invented, no orders are processed, and no payments are taken. Layout inspired by a public specialty-retailer website; no logos or text were copied.

Free shipping details (demo)

Standard shipping is free on merchandise orders of $99 or more after discounts. Smaller orders ship for $8.95. Express shipping is $14.95. Gift cards are delivered by email. This is invented demo policy text.
