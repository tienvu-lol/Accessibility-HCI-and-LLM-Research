    Learning center

    • Choosing waders

      Stockingfoot waders pair with boots and suit warm-to-cold water. Bootfoot waders are warmer and quicker to put on. Hip waders suit shallow creeks. Check the size & fit guide before you order.

    • Matching a rod to the water

      A 9-foot 5-weight handles most trout streams. Choose a lighter weight for small creeks and a heavier one for wind and bigger flies.

    • Caring for waders

      Rinse after each trip, hang them to dry inside out, and store them loosely folded. Small leaks can often be patched at home.
