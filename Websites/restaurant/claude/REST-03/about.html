About us

Our restaurant

Vane & Alder opened in an old dye works with one idea: cook what the valley gives, and say so plainly.

The room

Thirty-four seats, high windows and a long open pass. The room is deliberately quiet so that conversation, rather than music, fills the evening.

The cooking

The menu changes with the harvest from Lantern Fields and a handful of nearby producers. Dishes are built around a single idea and finished at the table.

The service

Dinner is a nine-course tasting menu and lasts roughly three hours. Tell us about dietary needs when you reserve; the kitchen plans around them.
