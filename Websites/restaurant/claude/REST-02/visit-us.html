Line tips

  • Weekdays: arrive by 10:30 am for a shorter wait.
  • Weekends: the line forms early. Bring water, a hat, and a folding chair.
  • Cannot wait? Use the demo pre-order tray.
  • Parties of 20 or more should look at catering.
