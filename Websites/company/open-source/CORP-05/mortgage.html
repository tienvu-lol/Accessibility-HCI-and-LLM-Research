Mortgage Options

Fixed-Rate Mortgage

Stability and predictability with a fixed interest rate for the life of the loan.

  • 15-year and 30-year terms
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • No prepayment penalties

Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM)

Lower initial rates that adjust over time based on market conditions.

  • 5/1, 7/1, and 10/1 options
  • Initial fixed period
  • Rate caps for protection

FHA Loans

Government-backed loans with lower down payment requirements.

  • 3.5% down payment
  • Flexible credit requirements
  • Lower closing costs

VA Loans

Exclusive benefits for veterans, active duty, and eligible spouses.

  • No down payment
  • No PMI
  • Competitive rates
