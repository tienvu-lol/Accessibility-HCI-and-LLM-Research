Accessibility statement

Our approach

This fictional site was built to a WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented baseline for a research study. That is a design target, not a claim of conformance; it has not been through a full audit.

Built-in features

  • Skip link, landmarks and a logical heading order
  • Keyboard operable menus, tabs, dialogs and accordions; Escape closes menus and dialogs
  • Labeled form fields with inline errors and an error summary
  • Reduced-motion support and reflow down to a narrow phone width

Known limitations

Testing so far was automated rules plus scripted keyboard checks. Screen reader and real-user testing have not been done.

Feedback: use the demo contact form (it does not send anything).
