    Accessibility

    How this research replica approaches accessible design and what has not been tested.

    Our approach

    • Semantic landmarks, a skip link and one main heading on each page.
    • Keyboard access for menus, tabs, dialogs, filters and forms, with Escape to close menus.
    • Labeled form fields, an error summary and inline errors.
    • Reduced-motion support and responsive layouts that reflow at narrow widths.

    Status: this is a baseline built toward WCAG 2.2 AA. It has not been audited, and no conformance is claimed. Manual screen reader testing was not done.
