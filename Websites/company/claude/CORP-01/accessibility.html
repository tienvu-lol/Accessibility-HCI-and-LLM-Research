Accessibility banking services

How Harborline (fictional) supports customers in branches, online and by phone.

Services

In branch

Seating, hearing loops, tactile-key ATMs and staff who can read documents aloud.

Online and mobile

Keyboard, screen-reader and zoom support are goals for this demo site; none are certified.

By phone

Relay calls (dial 711) and video interpreting by appointment.
