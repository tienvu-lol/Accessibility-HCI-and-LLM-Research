Overdraft services

Choose how Harborline handles a payment that exceeds your balance:

  • Linked savings transfer: $5 per transfer, no matter the amount.
  • Standby credit line: interest only on what you use.
  • Decline the payment: no overdraft fee.

Fees shown are illustrative.
