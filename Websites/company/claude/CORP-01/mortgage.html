Mortgage loans

Compare loan types, check rates and plan your payment. Nothing here is a real offer.

See sample ratesUse calculators

How a mortgage works

  1. Get prepared: check credit and gather documents.
  2. Choose a loan: compare fixed, adjustable and government-backed options.
  3. Apply: the application is a labeled demo here.

Loan types

Fixed-rate

The same rate and payment for 15 or 30 years.

Adjustable-rate

A lower starting rate that can change after the fixed period.

FHA and VA

Government-backed loans with flexible down-payment options.

Refinance

Replace your current loan to lower your rate, shorten your term or take cash out. Estimate savings with the calculators or sample rates.

Home equity

A HELOC or home equity loan lets you borrow against the value you have built, for improvements or large expenses. Terms in this replica are not offered.

Mortgage questions

Many loans allow less than 20%, but a smaller down payment can mean mortgage insurance.

The annual percentage rate includes the interest rate plus certain lender costs.
