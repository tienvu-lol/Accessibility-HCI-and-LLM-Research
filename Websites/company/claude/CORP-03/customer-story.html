Meridian Labs unified its build systems

A fictional hardware-software company moved three build systems onto one pipeline.

At a glance

Industry
Technology
Teams
14
Headline result
38% shorter pipeline time

The challenge

Firmware, cloud and mobile teams each owned a separate build system. Releases that touched all three needed a coordinator and a spreadsheet.

The approach

Meridian moved the three build flows onto shared pipeline templates and used approval rules to keep ownership clear across teams.

We stopped asking which system a change was waiting in. It was always the same place.

Fictional engineering director, Meridian Labs

The results

38% shorter

Median pipeline duration after consolidation.

1 workflow

Replacing three build systems and a coordination spreadsheet.

Fewer escalations

Cross-team release questions dropped after the first quarter.
