    Customer story (fictional)

    Brannock Aerospace cut release preparation from nine days to two

    An invented manufacturer of flight-systems software describes moving its delivery path into one workspace.

    Contact sales

    Results at a glance

    9 to 2

    days of release preparation (sample figure)

    4 to 1

    toolchains consolidated (sample figure)

    100%

    of releases with recorded approvals (sample figure)

    The challenge

    Release steps lived in separate tools, so every certification review began with a hunt for evidence.

    The approach

    The team moved reviews, builds and approvals to Cairn and let Relay attach evidence to each release automatically.
