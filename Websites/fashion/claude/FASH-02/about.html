About us

Vesperine is a fictional fashion house, written for an accessibility and usability research dataset.

Our story

Vesperine began as an imagined studio above a limestone hall, with a small team that cut patterns in the morning and wrote stories in the afternoon. The clothes are made to be worn often and mended well.

All names, products and stories on this site are invented. Any resemblance to a real brand is a result of following a common fashion-site structure.

What we care about

  • Wear it often

    Shapes that move from a Tuesday desk to a Saturday train without a change of shoes.

  • Mend it well

    Generous seam allowances, spare buttons and a repair guide with every coat.

  • Tell it plainly

    Stories in Vesper Notes are written to be read in five minutes and remembered for longer.

A short timeline

  1. 2019: The studio opens with three pattern cutters and one kettle.
  2. 2022: First runway show in the limestone hall.
  3. 2024: Vesper Notes launches as a weekly journal.
  4. 2026: Spring/Summer 2027 is shown in September.
