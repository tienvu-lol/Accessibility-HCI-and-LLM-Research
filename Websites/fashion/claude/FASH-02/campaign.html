Spring/Summer 2026: The Campaign

By Teodor Lind · Feb 2, 2026

Frame 1 of 6

The carousel does not advance on its own. Use the buttons, or the left and right arrow keys while focus is inside it.

A week at a glass greenhouse by the sea gave the campaign its light. We borrowed the building from a friend who grows tomatoes and keeps a very opinionated cat.

Every look was fitted on site, between watering rounds. Nothing was retouched beyond a little tidying of the hem.
