About and contact

Curbside Signal is a fictional magazine made as one static site in a research dataset on web accessibility and usability.

What is real and what is not

The layout and navigation patterns imitate a dense streetwear and culture news site. Everything else is invented: writers, labels, products, colourways, cities, events and dates. Imagery is abstract placeholder artwork and no photography is used.

What is left out on purpose

There is no shop, no cart, no account, no resale commentary and no advice on authenticating products. The release calendar is sample data only.
