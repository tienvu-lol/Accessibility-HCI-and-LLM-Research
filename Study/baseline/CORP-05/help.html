Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I reset my online banking password?

    Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Follow the instructions sent to your email.

  • How can I report a lost or stolen debit card?

    Call our 24/7 support line at 1-800-555-0199 or use the mobile app to freeze your card immediately.

  • What are the bank's operating hours?

    Branches are open Monday-Friday 9am-5pm and Saturday 9am-1pm. Online banking is available 24/7.

  • How do I set up a direct deposit?

    You can set up direct deposit through your employer's HR portal or by providing your account and routing number to your payer.

  • How can I contact a representative?

    Call 1-800-555-0199 or visit a branch. You can also use the secure message feature in online banking.
