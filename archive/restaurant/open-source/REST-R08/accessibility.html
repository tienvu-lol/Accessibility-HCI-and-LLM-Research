Accessibility Statement

We are committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. This statement outlines our ongoing efforts to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Measures to support accessibility

  • We integrate accessibility considerations into our design and development process.
  • All pages are built with semantic HTML5 and are navigable via keyboard.
  • We provide text alternatives for non‑text content and ensure sufficient color contrast.
  • Forms include associated labels, clear error messages, and focus management.
  • We respect user preferences for reduced motion and provide a visible focus indicator.
  • Skip links and ARIA landmarks are present to aid navigation.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this site. Please contact us if you encounter any barriers:

  • Email: accessibility@xianfoods.example
  • Phone: (212) 555-0199

Technical details

This website is built using only HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It is designed to work in modern browsers and when opened as a local file (file://). No external libraries or frameworks are used.

Limitations

While we strive for accessibility, some third‑party content (e.g., embedded social media feeds) may not fully conform. We encourage users to reach out if they encounter issues.
